The school has eleven pupils this year, which is the most it has had in a generation. The tide tables are pinned to the door of every shop, and they are consulted more often than the clock. Nobody locks their bicycles, though everybody locks their sheds, for reasons that are never explained. Children learn to read the weather from the colour of the water long before they learn it at school.
